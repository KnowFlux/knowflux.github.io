The Final Moment

Final raindrop falls

The world suspended in time

'fore it hits the ground

absolute silence

raindrop twinkles in the light

like a falling star

following its path:

aspiring to meet the earth

then to fall once more

Meaning

Suspended in the cycle

A reflection on cycles, suspended moments, and the beautiful inevitability of change. The raindrop becomes a symbol for pause, wonder, and the quiet acceptance that what falls must rise again, and what seems still is only a brief threshold between one motion and the next.
